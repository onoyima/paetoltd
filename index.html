About PA-ETOS LIMITED

PA-ETOS LIMITED is a leading project contractor with a strong reputation for delivering high-quality projects in various sectors.
Our team of experts has extensive experience in the under listed areas:

  • Road Construction
  • Building
  • Electrification / Solar Energy
  • General Supplies of Goods
  • Equipment Leasing
  • Build Operate Transfer
  • Contract Partnership, etc.

We ensure that our clients receive comprehensive solutions that meet their needs. We are committed to delivering top-notch services, adhering to quality standards, and ensuring client satisfaction. With a skilled team of professionals and a proven track record in the industry, we stand out as a reliable partner for projects of varying scales.

PA-ETOS LIMITED was established in September 1991 with a vision to provide exceptional services that prioritize client satisfaction, safety, and sustainability. Over the years, we have successfully completed numerous contract projects for government agencies and private entities, earning us a reputation as a trusted and reliable partner in the industry.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide innovative, sustainable, and safe construction solutions that exceed our clients' expectations, while maintaining the highest standards of quality, integrity, and social responsibility.
